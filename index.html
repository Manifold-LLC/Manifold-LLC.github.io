02  Selected work

Selected work

Research and delivery led by the people who founded Manifold. Some of it predates the firm, and customer names are withheld. We are glad to go into detail in conversation.

  1. Research · Maritime · Unpublished draft

    Re-finding ships that switch off AIS

    A multi-sensor tracker that re-finds dark vessels from one satellite pass to the next, scored against AIS it never saw.

    Read the case note
    Problem
    Vessels involved in illegal fishing, sanctions evasion and smuggling switch off AIS, the transponder that reports a ship's identity and position. Satellites still see them, but passes are hours apart, and by the next one a moving ship can be any of many unexplained detections.
    What was built
    A tracker that links detections from public radar, optical and night-light sensors (Sentinel-1 and NISAR, Sentinel-2 and Landsat, VIIRS) across the waters around Denmark and southern Norway. Each sensor has its own detector and attribute model, including heading and speed read from Sentinel-2's band delay and wake, and the motion model is learned from historical AIS. The system was scored against full-rate Danish and Norwegian AIS that it never saw.
    Result
    Ships at anchor or in port were re-found in most cases. Ships under way were re-found far less often, and the true next sighting was usually within a short ranked list of candidates. No published system was found that measures this problem, so the results would be a first public baseline once published. Changes to the tracking algorithm did not improve the moving-ship result; adding sensors did, which points to more looks per day as the lever. Results are from an unpublished draft.

  2. Research · Radar · Open source

    FastSAR: radar image formation on CPUs, GPUs and TPUs

    An open-source library that forms radar images faster than every open-source implementation tested, at equal or better accuracy.

    GitHub · PyPI · arXiv

    Read the case note
    Problem
    Every radar satellite returns raw phase histories that must be formed into images, and the constellation is growing fast. The open-source image-formation codes had never been compared on the same hardware against a common reference, and none ran on TPUs.
    What was built
    A library that forms SAR images on CPUs, NVIDIA GPUs and Google TPUs through one Python interface: exact backprojection, factorized backprojection and polar format, in several floating-point precisions, reading CPHD, SICD, Sentinel-1 and NISAR data. Every configuration was scored against a high-precision reference and priced at on-demand cloud rates, so a user can pick the accuracy, speed and cost they need.
    Result
    Faster than every open-source implementation tested, by orders of magnitude in most cases, at equal or better accuracy. A full commercial spotlight image forms in seconds on the lowest-priced cloud GPU for a fraction of a cent, and the comparison shows which precision and device to choose for interferometric, radiometric or quick-look use. Written with a co-author at Penn State's Applied Research Laboratory. The code is public under the MIT license on GitHub and installs from PyPI; the paper is on arXiv and submitted to IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing.

  3. Research · Vessel movement · Under review

    A foundation model for where vessels go

    One model, pretrained on public AIS traffic, that forecasts where a vessel will be as a probability map.

    Read the case note
    Problem
    Forecasting where a vessel will be, and what it is doing, underlies collision avoidance, search and rescue, port planning and fisheries enforcement. Each of those tasks has had its own small model.
    What was built
    A single model pretrained on years of public AIS traffic that forecasts a vessel's position as a probability map at any time horizon, with small task heads on the frozen model for the questions analysts ask of a track.
    Status
    A paper is under review. We are able to discuss the approach, not the results, prior to publication.

  4. Delivery · Generative AI

    Generative AI for CUI at a Navy UARC

    A language-model assistant over Controlled Unclassified Information, delivered to production on Amazon Bedrock in an Impact Level 4 AWS GovCloud environment.

    Read the case note

    What was done
    Designed and delivered the system on Amazon Bedrock inside an Impact Level 4 AWS GovCloud environment, integrated with the laboratory's directory service (LDAP), with TLS on every connection.
    Outcome
    Delivered to production on the laboratory's controlled network.

03  Approach

How we work

We are a small firm and we run engagements like one. These are the rules we hold ourselves to.

  1. The people you meet do the work.

    The practitioner who scopes an engagement does the work. Nothing is handed to junior staff after the kickoff, and nobody is proposed to you who will not be in the room.

  2. A written scope and a fixed deliverable.

    Every engagement begins with a clearly defined scope and ends with a tangible deliverable, from technical assessments and requirements packages to working systems. We commit to realistic timelines and take on only what we can confidently deliver.

  3. The right AI for the mission.

    Our recommendations start from the mission and match it to the AI approach that fits, from foundation models to smaller specialized models. We say plainly what each will take to build and to run.

  4. Current with the research, and with the hardware.

    We read the papers and run the models ourselves, so when a vendor says its model does something we can check.

  5. Cleared.

    Everyone at Manifold holds a TS/SCI clearance with counterintelligence (CI) polygraph and actively supports the Intelligence Community.

  6. No conflicts of interest.

    Our independence is fundamental to how we operate. We separate advisory and implementation work, never competing for contracts we help shape or evaluate.

About Manifold

Manifold LLC is a Virginia small business founded by two practitioners who have spent their careers inside Intelligence Community research and acquisition.
