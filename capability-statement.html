Independent AI/ML guidance for Intelligence Community program offices

Manifold is a small Virginia-based AI/ML firm founded by senior technical leaders with firsthand experience supporting the Intelligence Community. Our backgrounds span trusted-agent and SETA roles, from prototyping and technical requirements to source selections and strategic investment decisions. We help government organizations determine what to buy, what to build, and how to deploy AI/ML capabilities in secure environments. We provide independent advisory services or hands-on development, maintaining a strict separation between the two for each customer.

Research

Maritime domain awareness · Research

Built and measured a tracker that re-finds ships that have switched off AIS from one satellite pass to the next, over the waters around Denmark and southern Norway, using public radar, optical and night-light sensors (Sentinel-1, NISAR, Sentinel-2, Landsat, VIIRS), a motion model learned from historical AIS, and scoring against withheld Danish and Norwegian AIS. No published system was found that measures this problem; the results would be a first public baseline once published. Unpublished draft.

Synthetic aperture radar · Open-source software

FastSAR, a library that forms SAR images on CPUs, NVIDIA GPUs and Google TPUs through one Python interface (exact and factorized backprojection, polar format; CPHD, SICD, Sentinel-1 and NISAR inputs). Every configuration scored against a high-precision reference and priced at cloud rates: faster than every open-source implementation tested, by orders of magnitude in most cases, at equal or better accuracy. Open source (MIT) at github.com/saulpingerman/FastSAR and on PyPI as fastsar; preprint arXiv:2610.33932, submitted to IEEE TGRS.

Vessel movement · Foundation model

A single model pretrained on public AIS traffic that forecasts a vessel's position as a probability map at any time horizon, with small task heads on the frozen model for analysts' questions. Paper under review.

Led by the firm's founders. Code, preprints and drafts are available where their status allows.
