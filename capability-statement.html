Independent AI/ML advice for Intelligence Community and Department of Defense programs

Manifold LLC is a Virginia small business run by cleared practitioners with about ten years inside IC acquisition and research programs. We help program offices decide what to build, what to buy, and how to get it running on accredited networks.

Core competencies

Strategy and roadmaps

Foundation-model strategy, capability roadmaps, build-versus-buy analysis, and technology forecasts. Technical SME on competitive acquisitions and BAA panels; AI/ML training curricula for sponsor staff.

Acquisition and compute

Technical requirements, RFP and SOW language, source-selection criteria, and vendor evaluation. GPU and TPU cluster sizing and cost models across AWS, Azure, and Google GovCloud or on-premises hardware.

Program assessment

Independent technical evaluation of AI/ML programs, maturity and risk assessment, milestone and design reviews, and sponsor-facing performance metrics.

Secure generative AI

Reference architectures for CUI at Impact Level 4 and 5, retrieval-augmented generation design and delivery, model and vendor selection in GovCloud, evaluation plans, and bias audits.

Past performance (principals)

Navy UARC · Generative AI

Designed and delivered a production retrieval-augmented generation system for CUI on Amazon Bedrock in an Impact Level 4 AWS GovCloud environment, integrated with the laboratory's LDAP directory, TLS throughout.

Geospatial foundation models

Architected the modeling and training effort for foundation models over overhead and geospatial data, working directly with Google and NVIDIA engineering teams on data pipelines, model design, and compute planning.

IC acquisition

About ten years on the government side of Intelligence Community acquisition: requirements, source-selection and BAA evaluation, AI/ML training design, and senior-leadership briefings.

  • Hands-on with both acquisition and model training
